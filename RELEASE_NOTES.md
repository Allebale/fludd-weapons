Mod API: 1.8

For the stable game with mod API 1.8 (launcher Stable 1.2.0 and newer). On an older game (mod API 1.7), use v1.0.3.

**1.4.1: everything from the 1.3.1 and 1.3.2 pre-releases is back** (1.4.0 left it out by mistake), together with 1.4.0's ice goop.

### Shooter aim in third person
- **Hold L to aim like a modern third-person shooter.** The camera moves over Mario's shoulder, you aim with the mouse (or the C-stick), and you can still walk in any direction and jump while aiming.
- Uses the same crosshair and colors as the First Person mod.
- The water lands on the crosshair at close and medium range, and drops short at long range like FLUDD's normal stream.
- The Rifle, SMG and Bazooka shoot exactly where the crosshair points.
- Mario turns round properly when walking backwards and dives freely while aiming.
- When you let go of L, the normal camera faces the way you were aiming.
- New settings: **mouse speed**, **field of view**, **inverted** (up and down, left and right, or both), **crosshair left or right**, and **crosshair color**.

### Coin Magnet
- **Enemies you defeat with the Rifle while looking through the scope send their coins flying to you.** On by default.

### Rocket jump controller button
- Choose which controller button does rocket jumps (A by default).

### Goop on 3D Grass (Remastered builds)
- Electric and Black goop press 3D Grass flat, and Fire goop burns it.

### Ice goop (from 1.4.0)
- Black goop can turn to ice when another mod asks for it (the Christmas Additions mod does): white-blue ice, just as slippery and fast, and enemies it touches freeze solid before they are defeated.

**Install:** unzip into your `mods` folder and turn it on in the Mods menu (F1).
